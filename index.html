My favourite animal : The Sloth

The sloth is my favourite animal because it is cute, calm, and very slow. Sloths live mainly in the tropical rainforests of Central and South America and spend most of their lives hanging from trees.

Three Facts About Sloths

  • Sloths are known for their slow movement, which helps them conserve energy.
  • They have a unique symbiotic relationship with algae that grows on their fur, providing camouflage.
  • Sloths can hold their breath for up to 40 minutes, allowing them to stay underwater for extended periods.
